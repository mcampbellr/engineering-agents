## SCORED CHECKS (code/repo-detectable)

### 1. AI disclosure to users (HIGH if user-facing chatbot with none)
Users must be told they are interacting with AI (EU AI Act Art. 50 chatbot
transparency). Look for disclosure text in UI/system copy.
```bash
grep -rniE '(you.?re (chatting|talking) (to|with) (an )?ai|ai assistant|powered by ai|this is an ai|generated by ai|ai-generated)' \
  . --include='*.tsx' --include='*.ts' --include='*.jsx' --include='*.html' --include='*.md' \
  -n 2>/dev/null | grep -v 'node_modules' | head || echo "No AI disclosure copy found"
```

### 2. Terms & Privacy coverage (MEDIUM)
Terms of Service / Privacy Policy present AND mentioning AI use, data processing,
and subprocessors (the LLM provider).
```bash
find . -iname '*terms*' -o -iname '*privacy*' -o -iname '*tos*' 2>/dev/null | grep -v 'node_modules' | head
grep -rniE '(privacy policy|terms of service|data processing|subprocessor|third.?part.*(openai|anthropic|google))' \
  . --include='*.md' --include='*.mdx' --include='*.html' -l 2>/dev/null | grep -v 'node_modules' | head \
  || echo "No T&C/Privacy referencing AI/data processing found"
```

### 3. AI-generated content labeling (MEDIUM)
Where the system publishes generated content (esp. deepfakes / public-interest
text), it should be labeled/identifiable (EU AI Act Art. 50).

### 4. PII handling & retention/deletion (MEDIUM)
If user personal data reaches the LLM: retention limits, deletion/opt-out, and
data-minimization. Cross-reference PII redaction from `step_07_ai_security.md`.
```bash
grep -rniE '(data.?retention|retention.?period|right.?to.?(be.?forgotten|erasure)|delete.?(user|account).?data|opt.?out|do.?not.?train|zdr|zero.?data.?retention)' \
  . -n 2>/dev/null | grep -v 'node_modules\|test' | head || echo "No retention/deletion/opt-out handling found"
```

### 5. Lawful basis / DPIA references (LOW)
For PII processing, any documented lawful basis, DPIA/FRIA, or consent capture.

### 6. Human oversight for consequential decisions (MEDIUM)
Decisions with legal/material effect on people should have human oversight /
appeal (cross-reference Area 4 HITL).

## SEVERITY
- HIGH: user-facing chatbot with no AI disclosure.
- MEDIUM: no T&C/Privacy covering AI/data; no content labeling where required; no retention/deletion for PII; no oversight for consequential decisions.
- LOW: no documented lawful basis / DPIA.

## SCORING HINTS
Base 100. Deduct: no disclosure (user-facing) −25; no T&C/Privacy AI coverage −15;
no content labeling −10; no PII retention/deletion −15; no lawful-basis/DPIA −5; no oversight −10.
Bonus: AI disclosure present +5; privacy policy names AI subprocessors + retention +5.
If the system is internal/batch and non-user-facing, mark disclosure/labeling N/A
(do not deduct) and score the remaining items.

## ADVISORY ANNEX (NON-SCORED — feeds report Section 13)

Emit an actionable, jurisdiction-aware checklist. Prefix with the disclaimer:
**"This is orientation only, not legal advice; consult qualified counsel and do
not treat any EU AI Act risk classification here as binding."**

Cover:
- **EU AI Act** — likely risk tier (prohibited / high-risk / limited / minimal);
  for limited-risk chatbots: Art. 50 duty to disclose AI interaction and label
  AI-generated content (transparency obligations apply from Aug 2026). GPAI
  obligations if shipping a model. Extraterritorial: applies if outputs affect
  EU users. Penalties up to €35M / 7% turnover.
- **GDPR** (if processing EU personal data) — lawful basis, data minimization,
  transparency, security; DPIA (Art. 35) and FRIA (Art. 27) for high-risk;
  provider as processor/subprocessor + DPA; retention & deletion; cross-border
  transfer of prompts.
- **Provider terms** — training opt-out / zero-data-retention where required;
  usage-policy compliance; attribution/copyright of generated content.
- **Website/product copy** — T&C clause covering AI limitations & no-warranty on
  outputs; Privacy Policy naming the AI subprocessor and data flows; visible AI
  disclosure in the chat UI; content labels.

Present each as: item · required? (Yes/If-applicable) · detected in repo? (Yes/No/N/A) · action.
